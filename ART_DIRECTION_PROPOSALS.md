# Dead Letter Office — art-direction proposals

The three directions share the same seeded gameplay state and handlers. They differ in composition, material language, typography, and how an anomaly breaks the normal world.

## A — Sorting Loom

**Thesis.** The sorting machine is the scene: three continuous belts carry physical evidence toward the filing desk. The current Archive Theatre identity remains recognizable, but the parcels become the primary visual vocabulary.

**Why it belongs.** Postal machinery, worn green paint, brass guides, and paper forms make the institution tangible without pretending to be a generic dashboard.

**Gameplay communication.** Belt position implies urgency; silhouettes identify parcel archetypes; the inspector and filing row explain consequence and route in one horizontal read.

**Strongest element.** A continuous rail system where every parcel breaks the surrounding rhythm in a meaningful way.

**Risk.** It can still read as a sophisticated control surface if the side rails become too loud.

## B — Telegraph Nocturne

**Thesis.** The game becomes a night telegraph stage: one illuminated case file is held in a void, and incoming parcels arrive like signal fragments around it. Anomaly states appear as registration errors, ink bloom, and impossible alignment.

**Why it belongs.** Dead letters are messages from the wrong side of time. The telegraph metaphor gives the supernatural a visual grammar based on transmission failure rather than decoration.

**Gameplay communication.** The active case is the brightest object. The three destinations are signal terminals; cards are interventions that reroute the message.

**Strongest element.** An anomaly can visibly mis-register the scene without adding another badge or panel.

**Risk.** Atmosphere can outrun affordance, especially on small screens.

## C — Registry Room

**Thesis.** A clear, cool filing room where forms, tabs, and red stamps make the rules legible. The frame is quiet; the case assembly is explicit; the three destinations are always visible.

**Why it belongs.** The institution is a bureaucracy first, and the supernatural is most effective when it interrupts an otherwise believable records system.

**Gameplay communication.** Case slots read as a single form, destination tabs stay persistent, and danger is carried by red routing language plus silhouette.

**Strongest element.** Immediate first-time clarity: what is selected, what can be added, and where the case goes are all visible at once.

**Risk.** It may feel less haunted if the anomalous marks are not restrained but unmistakable.

## Implementation notes

- The selector on the title screen changes only the visual class; state, seed, balance, and handlers remain shared.
- Candidate A is the incumbent champion baseline.
- Candidate B is the radical challenger.
- Candidate C is the readability challenger and the recommended direction for further work, pending user approval.
