# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players looking for a short, replayable supernatural postal roguelike. They sit down for a night shift and need to make fast, legible filing decisions while discovering strange rules.

## Product Purpose

Dead Letter Office is an offline game about sorting mail that should not exist. Success means the player understands the immediate filing decision, can act without a tutorial wall, and feels the institution's pressure and humor.

## Positioning

The game's mechanism is a three-lane postal puzzle where parcels carry supernatural properties, cards manipulate the evidence, and docket mode asks the player to assemble several parcels into one case before filing it.

## Operating Context

The player works a night shift at a supernatural postal department. They inspect incoming parcels, use cards, choose a route, and end the turn so the belts advance. Russian is the product language. The game runs locally in a modern browser with localStorage saves.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing playable HTML/CSS/JavaScript game must remain functional.
- Docket Registry / Реестр дел is the primary discovery surface for this phase.
- A representative state must show incoming parcels, an active case, three filing destinations, a card hand, resources, turn pressure, an anomaly, and actionable feedback.
- No balance rewrite is in scope.
- The visual exploration must remain recoverable and must not silently replace the production presentation.

## Brand Commitments

The name Dead Letter Office, the night-shift postal setting, dry institutional humor, supernatural anomalies, and Russian UI copy are binding. The identity should feel like an original indie game rather than a web dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand

The playable implementation is in `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, and `assets.svg`. The art-direction brief is supplied in the user attachment and is the source for this phase. No external commercial proof or sourced art should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The object silhouette should teach the rule before the paragraph does.
2. A filing decision is the hero interaction.
3. Supernatural effects are interruptions in a coherent postal world.
4. Humor belongs in the language and details, not in sacrificed readability.
5. Visual experimentation must preserve a playable, comparable state.
